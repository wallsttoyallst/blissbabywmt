2. Meta Pixel, Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Website uses the Meta Pixel, an analytics and advertising tool provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. ("Meta"). The Meta Pixel sets cookies and collects information about your visit, such as the pages you view and the product links you click, together with identifiers that Meta may associate with your Meta account if you have one. We use this information to measure the performance of our advertising on Meta platforms (such as Facebook and Instagram), to build audiences for our advertising, and to show you ads for Bliss Baby products that may interest you. Information collected through the Meta Pixel is also processed by Meta under its own privacy policy, available at facebook.com/privacy/policy.

Our hosting provider also keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, request time and pages requested) that help us operate and secure the Website.

Most browsers accept cookies by default. You can set your browser to refuse cookies or to alert you when a cookie is being sent, and you can clear cookies at any time through your browser settings.
